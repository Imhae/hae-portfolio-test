Core Flow 02 · Offers, Negotiation & Chat

Turning negotiation into a transaction you can track.

The challenge was not chat itself, but keeping price, intent, and transaction status clear as the conversation changes in real time.

Clearly distinguished roles

The buyer makes an offer; the seller responds in the same context.

The offer is a structured state

Price, timing, and responses never get lost in the chat.

Both sides see the next step

Accept, counter, or decline are presented consistently.
